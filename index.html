Free Online Games, No Download

Darky Play is a small collection of free online games that run straight in your browser. There is nothing to install, no account to make and no app store involved — open a game and it starts. Every game works on a phone, a tablet and a computer, with touch controls on small screens and keyboard controls on big ones.

The games are original, but most of them are built on ideas you already know. If you came looking for a pacman game, a snake game, a flappy bird style game, a tic tac toe board or a brick breaker, there is one here.

What you can play

  • Neon Pacman — a pac man style maze chase over 10 levels, with power pellets and ghosts that get fancier as you climb.
  • Happy Bird — a flappy bird style tap game. One button, four medals, endless pipes.
  • Pro Snake — the classic snake game, the one everyone remembers from old phones.
  • Pikachu Breakout — a brick breaker in the arkanoid tradition: bounce, clear, survive.
  • Pic Pac Poe — tic tac toe (noughts and crosses) against a computer that sharpens every time you beat it.
  • Stratosfer Merge — a suika style merge puzzle. Drop orbs, double their value, do not overflow.
  • Fired Space Shooter — a space invaders style shooter with waves and a boss.
  • Prime Escape — an endless runner across 10 night-time levels with police on your tail.
  • Long Towers — a block stacking game. Every miss slices the tower thinner.
  • NBA Stack — basketball badges balanced into a tower that leans when you misjudge it.
  • Word Catch — a word search puzzle against the clock.
  • IQur — a 60 second maths quiz that scores your speed and accuracy.
  • Holepringo — a hole io style eating game. Drag, swallow, grow your score.
  • Spyro Taxi — a 3D dodging game. Jump the traffic, keep the run alive.

Questions

Are these games really free?
Yes. Every game is free to play, with no payment, no trial and nothing locked behind a purchase.

Do I need to download or install anything?
No. They are browser games. Tap a game and it loads — no app, no plugin, no sign up.

Do they work on a phone?
Yes. Every game has touch controls and is built to fit a phone screen, in portrait, without zooming.

Are they suitable for kids?
Yes. The games are simple arcade and puzzle games with no violence, no chat and no way for players to contact each other.

Do my scores get saved?
Your best score for each game is kept on your own device, and the leaderboard shows the best runs from everyone who plays on it.
